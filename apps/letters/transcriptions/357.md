O.U. January 13th 1944

Dear Parents.

Greetings from here and today I had mail from you. Letters # 36-37- 40 and 44 and a card from Dec 27th The Parcel from Hurt Hebestreit and Ruhe I did get too,. The letters that you had mailed to my old address will be send on to me. We will stay here until Feb 12th and then back to the old unit. I might get furlough than, let us hope that the furlough restrictions are over. I will close now , I am tired. Greetings and hope to see you soon.

Werner.

From Pvt Werner Ketschau, FP # 58 902 = Sharpshooters Comp 81

To: family Erich Ketschau, Kleinmélsen-Erfurt Thurg.
