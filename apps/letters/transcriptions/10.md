Dear Vati

Will write to you again and tell you what I got from the Easter Bunny, a bag with eggs, 10 small green candies, and a small bar of

chocolate with a boy and an elephant on the label, 3 more boiled eggs. Traude brought down her dolls pram and we went for a walk because spring is here now, I would love to come to Mühlberg and fetch you.

Greetings and see you soon

Hellmut
