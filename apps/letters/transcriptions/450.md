June 22™ 1941

Dear Beloved !

What do you say now? Did you hear the Proclamation by our Führer at 6.30 AM on the Radio? This damn Russian swines, they will find out what they let themselves into. They will be

eliminated in short order and then our back is free , the British would have loved it to bring the Russian Bandits on their side, just to please the stinking Jews , it has always been their aim. Attack is the best defense , I am well, are you looking forward to that trip to Thuringia?

Heartfelt Greetings and Kisses from your Pappi.

From: ?

To Mrs Ilse Braun, Bad Pyrmont Altenaustr 4
