Russia Aug 28th 1943

Dear Beloved All !

I have to write despite the fact that is difficult to write letters in the middle of combat. I am well, but the war seems to have turned into a totalitarian struggle It is a warm day and the enemy planes attack uu in huge swarms. Write often but do not worry about me, just think that these hard times will be over one day. Anyone who has not been on the front would not know what it means to be here . It isa battle of the nerves too, because we never get a decent rest to recuperate. Well, let us see what will happen. A minor wound and a hospital bed would be the ideal thing. Many Greetings from your “

Heinrich.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier FP # 10331 = Art.Regt 117

To: H.Bredemeier Rehren A.O. # 12 Near Rinteln, Weser.
