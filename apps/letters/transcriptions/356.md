O.U. January 2nd 1944 raue

Dear Parents.

Sending you Greetings from here and hope all is well at home, Thank you for the card from Dec 17th and letter # 35 . Also thank you for the parcel, with Pepper cake, Sausage, and Butter. Also had a parcel from Udestedt with cake and apples, if it had come a day later, I would have missed it, as we had moved out the next day. I wrote the Teacher in

Mittelhausen , my teeth are ok now, and here is now the best Spring weather .I did not get the cigarettes yet, tomorrow we are going to Bernstein, I don’t know where that is. How was New Years Eve at home? We had enough to drink so everything stays the same here. I will close now sending you Greetings

Your Werner.
