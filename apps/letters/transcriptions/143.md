Sunday Oct 11th 1942

Parents and Grand Dad.

Here I am sitting in the rail Road station in Dnjperpetrowsk and try to write a few things. I never thought that our retreat ended up that far back, but now I hope they bring me into a hospital to Poland well, anywhere nearer to Germany, . Our train has 400 wounded and sick soldiers on board, and there is no room to take anymore on. So therefore I am hoping to go further back. We in cattle cars and hoping for a delousing station, I can not tell you more and no news from me.

So sending you Greetings

Heinrich.

Letter was written on his way from the front to Hospital in Rothenburg ob der Tauber in Germany.
