Rothenburg Feb 11th 1943

Dear Beloved All.

Today I had another letter from you and a parcel thank you very much, Everything was good of course. Have you got the parcel I mailed on Feb 1st? I can not tell you much from here. So how are the cows at home? Are they all well, How much Milk do you produce , any calves yet or coming soon? I suppose you cold hold 6-7 Milkers anyway. You got some workers now on the farm. Maybe next month I can get furlough and help with the sowing . Once I am out of hospital and back with the reserve Army, I have.a chance of a long furlough so you put in an application for a temprarly release for me. Make sure you get some important person to sign it too. AFTER New year I have put 40 pounds in weight on and I am now 180 pounds and nobody is that heavy here but do not tell everybody. It is possible that I arrive in Rehren Obernkirchen on Feb 25th with a furlough Pass , Franz Struckmeier is also on furlough in Gottingen -Weende. For now many Greetings

Heinrich.

From: Pvt 1st Class Heinrich Bredemeier, Rothenburg 0.d. Tauber Hospital Bären

To: Family Heinrich Bredemeier,Rehren A.O. #12 Near Rinteln Weser
