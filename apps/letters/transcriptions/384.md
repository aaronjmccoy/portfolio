In the field Nov 15"" 1944

Dear Parents

Greetings from here, hope all is well at home. It will only be a card today I don’t know what I shall write about, snowing tonight and yesterday we had a hard frost. It all turned to mud again now. No other news Greetings to you from

Werner.

From: Pvt 1st Class Werner Ketschau FP # 27 658 Panzer Grenadier Regt 6

To. Fam Erich Ketschau, Kleinmdélsen, Erfurt / Thur.
