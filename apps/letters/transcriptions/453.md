Auschwitz Jan 24th 1943

My dear Wife.

I am writing to you from Auschwitz and this is my 3rd letter and

no answer came. I am worried . What is new at home? What are you doing?

You got a job? Write soon, I am very well and in good health, small parcels

are permitted to be send, I would like some fat and sweetener. The total

weight must not exceed 250 Grams and I beg you to send these parcels a bit

more often. Many Greetings and Kisses also to my daughter Basia and Irene

, also give my Greetings to my Mother, mother in Law , Brother in Law and

family. I expect a fast response.

Starck

From: protective Prisoner Camp Walter Starcklaks B; Nov 6th 1906
