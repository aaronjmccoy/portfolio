March 16th 1941

Dear Parents and grand dad !

I don’t know what to write about but I got the parcel with the washing. But the 20 Marks have not come yet. I have been out all week out in the field and next Wednesday is the big inspection coming up . I don’t know about my furlough yet , I got new horses now, but not very well trained on a Wagon. A photo I enclose of them, these are our best Horses, > Dear Father, I got your Card from Hannover and I am glad to hear that the tractor will be repaired. Most of the Troops have left here but Para troops hace arrived now. Don’t send me any sausage for now Greetings Heinrich.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier, FP # 10331 Art Regt 117

To: H. Bredemeier Rehren,A.O. #12 Schaumburg.
