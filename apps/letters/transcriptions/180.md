Braunschweig June 10th 1943

Dear All at home !

Just a short letter while I got the time. I have now packed all my things and we soon off to the new camp . I have just applied for furlough s well, for Whitsun 3 days and no train to be used. I take that risk I can return with my bicycle if you bring it up to standart at home. I have to risk it or I never will come. Fritz siemann is with us again, Its hope my furlough application goes through. Have a happy Whitsun for now Greetings

Heinrich

From H.Bredemeier , March Battery Art Regt 31 Braunschweig

To: Family Bredemeier, Rehren, A.O. #12 near Rinteln Weser
