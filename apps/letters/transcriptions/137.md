Eastern Front Sept 11th 1942

Dear All beloved !

Yesterday I got 4 parcels and a letter from Aug 16th and thank you very much. I am still ok, and I hear you have many good workers at the farm. That guarantees you that you get it done. I would love to participate in the harvest but unless the Russians givge in, there is no hope for furlough. Yes, the parcels are coming always on time, but Dear Mother, you can send some Sugar and Pudding Powder again, . Yesterday I transferred 120 Marks to you, I hope that you soon will get the electric Cooker and the fertilizer spinner, how is the young Horse Doing? Will it be ready for work in the Fall? I will close now

Greetings Heinrich.

From: Pvt 1st Class Heinrich Bredemeier, FP # 10331 Artillery Regt 117

To: Family Heinrich Bredemeier, Rehren A.O.# 12, near Rinteln, Weser.
