Easter 1944

My dear Little Karl.

I was surprised to get mail from you from the RAD Labor service camp. So at last they got you too how do you like it?

“Very Good” ? But don’t worry, that time will pass too. Make sure you are at home when I come on furlough and polish the spade too , don’t forget. But don’t worry, your asses will be tied up too . Dear Karl, we are moving again so wait until I write again. Happy Easter I am wishing you.

From your Friend Fritz

From: Sailor Fritz Ralfs FP # M 45127 = Front U- Boat -BdU Commander of all Submarines

To: Karl Schweinebart , RAD Abtlg 8/11, Bialistok, GG.
