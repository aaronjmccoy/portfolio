In the east Aug 29th 1944

Dear Parents.

Sending you my Greetings I am well and hope you too, I did not get any mail yesterday. The weather is good here, did you get the 2 parcels? Is all well at home?

Greetings from Werner.

From: Pvt 1st Class Werner Ketschau, FP # 27658 = Panzer Grenadier Regt
