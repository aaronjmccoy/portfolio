defarch 9th 1947 gs

Dear All!

I arrived here ok, and when I got here I found 4 of your parcels on my bed. I have plenty to eat now and in addition I got the Cake

coupons thia morning I was at the Rifle Range already, and did a very good score. Included is a picture , someone just happened to take a photo of us when I rode the Staff Sgt horse out Father, Make _ sure the tractor is repaired All the Best Heinrich.

From: Heinrich Bredemeier, Art Reserve Abtlg 216 4th Battery,

Göttingen

To: H. Bredemeier Rehren,A.O. #12 Schaumburg.
