Zgierz Sept 30th 1942

Dear Parents and Werner.

Greetings from here and I hope all is well and so I am .I have had no mail for several days, today we had a big inspection here by the Top Division Doctor. I might not being released soon from here, until ther is a new train of wounded from the front arrives. That wont suit me much, I want to get back to my Company soon. How far you with the potatoes? Do you have help from the other Ketschaus? How are the Ritter’s doing? Write soon, I will close now, sending Greetings to all.

Your Artur.
