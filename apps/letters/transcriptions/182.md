Breslau June 20th 1943

Dear Beloved at Home !

We are all in transit now, and the train has stopped and I can write a quick letter. I am using a wooden box for a table, there are 35 of us on this cattle car and not much room. I am well, and I can tell you not to worry about me..-I will get there wherever they take us and get back again. It will take some time We soldiers have to adapt to all situations . I am already missing the good beer from Braunschweig in a few days we will enter Poland and soon after we will be in Russia again . I will close now so let us look forward to a victorious 4th year of this war and Greetings from your

Heinrich.

From :Pvt 1st Class Heinrich Bredemeier in transit

To Family Heinrich Bredemeier , Rehren A.O. #12 near Rinteln Weser
