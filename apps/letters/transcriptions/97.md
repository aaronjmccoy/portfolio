Russia March 15th 1942

Dear Parents and Grand Dad !

Yesterday I got 7 parcels again and so nice to get them and I thank you very much. The home baked cakes were very good and with the Pudding Powder I made some good tasty Pudding. Today is is bitterly cold agin, 20 Minus C Major Lange has written to me and also Almund Fink. I answered them all. You wrote about a watch for 50 Marks from France but I don’t think it will be good to send that to me. Keep it for Father, he has no watch, I live by the stand of sun here. I hope that you had my letters by now, I am still ok, tonight I will go to see a front theater show. This will be presented by Ukrainian. The Theater is 2 Kilometers behind the front line.

Greetings to you all Heinrich.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier FP # 10331 = Art.Regt 117

To: H.Bredemeier Rehren A.O. # 12 Near Rinteln, Weser.
