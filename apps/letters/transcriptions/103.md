Eastern Front April 5th 1942

Dear All at home!

Today is Easter Sunday, Christmas in the snow and now Easter as well. I already wrote a few letters today. Today it is thawing but new snow will fall on top of it. Let us look forward to May, and the winter will leave this cursed country. I hope that you are all well, , I too feel a bit better after the Rheumatism bout. Please send me envelopes and writing paper, how is your weather there? Here they building a narrow gauge Rail Road so we can get supplies without going many miles on muddy roads.

For now all the very best.

Heinrich.
