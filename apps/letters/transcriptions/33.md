April 7th 1941

Dear All!

Today I got your long letter and you said that you already have sown some oats . There is no chance yet for furlough but you could do some enquieries at the Battery Command why I am denied the promised furlough. Easter I have to stay here so I wish you a happy Easter. We shall leave Bergen soon I have not seen the Dentist yet, and my sick report was not acceptet because I have to be present at the field training. It seems they can not work without me.let me know whether you get all the photos from me. For now all the best.

Heinrich.
