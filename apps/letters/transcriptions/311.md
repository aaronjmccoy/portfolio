March 18th 1943

Dear Parents.

Greetings from here, I am well and hope you too. I had no mail from you so far, and I am 5 weeks gone from home now. At least I should have had some mail from you. What is the matter then? Please write soon so I know what is going on. Lets hope for a reunion.

Your Artur.
