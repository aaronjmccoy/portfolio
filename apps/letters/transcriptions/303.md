Feb 20th 1943

Dear Werner. ;

Just a few words today with Greetings from the east. I am well hope the same from you. We are still in the siding of the Rail Road Station in Orscha and we in a terrible Blizzard and the Cold is killing us. Do you write home on time? For now

Greetings from

Your Brother Artur.
