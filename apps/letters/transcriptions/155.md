Rothenburg Dec 9th 1942

Dear Parents and Grand Dad !

Today I got the letter from Dec 6th and thank you very much. I am well, considering the situation , the fever has gone and I hope that I be fit by Christmas . I have gotten used to the bed now, and the nurses spoil us. . You said you wanted to visit me here, please stay were you are, there is no need for that and you have loads of work at home anyway, and traveling 400 Kilometers just to visit me when I am not bad is silly. I have plenty od entertainment here, and soon I will get my home furlough anyway. I shall be at home a few weeks I hope. Don’t think I would love to get back to Russia, and look forward to another winter there. You carry on with your work and do mine until I came home. Greetings from

Your son Heinreich

From: Pvt 1st Class H. Bredemeier Rothenburg o.d. T. Hospital Baren

To: Family H. Bredemeier , Rehren A.O. #12 Near Rinteln -Weser
