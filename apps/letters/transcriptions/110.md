Eastern Front May 4th 1942

Dear all t Home !

Today I received the cards from April 17th and 18th and a few parcels, and many thanks. Glad that you have sown the oats now, and that all is well and the weather is good. Also that the tractor is going well. What about Lt Zuttenmeister , is he reported missing or was he killed at at he front? Never get to worried about me, I am still well as always, , something else, when you write open Post cards, do not write how worried you are ok? L. Gellermann has been taken to a Hospital in Poland , he has kidney trouble at least he got away from here with his life. One Year ago I was home the last time on furlough, I got used to the absence if only the war here in the east was ending. And we can then put the British in a vice. For now all the very best.

Heinrich.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier, FP # 10331 = Artillery Regt 117

To: Family Bredemeier, Rehren A.O. # 12, near Rinteln, Weser.
