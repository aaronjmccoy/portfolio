In the East Oct 14th 1941

Dear Mia!

Today I got the sweet greeting in the parcel from you dated Sept 9th, it took 4 weeks to get here, some mail takes ages , some is fast. Today I also got a letter from my Brother who is in Africa, the poor boy has not had any furlough for over a year. He is still waiting now, but I am not lucky too, but I hope for furlough in the winter. I am maybe 4 000 Kilometers from home now, and it must be punishment to sit on a train for that distance. But the things we do to get home on furlough. But you can be assured Mia, I will not miss Delmenhorst when I come. I hope you have some time for me then. Nothing has changed here so I send you Greetings

Your Ernst.

From. Sgt Ernst Kühlen FP # 12 829 = Luftwaffe Base 207/1

To: Miss Mia Klümann, Delmenhorst/ Olbg. Weserstr 27.
