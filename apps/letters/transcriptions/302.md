Feb 19th 194 J

Dear Parents.

Today I am writing a few lines again. We are once again on a train and we are on the rail Station in Orscha. We left last night and then we pulled in to Orscha , because the Partisans had blown the track so we returned here. Now we are told we will stay here until Monday. The weather is not very nice here, since early this morning a Blizzard is howling, a storm like you would never see at home and the biting cold with it. We will stay in the car in this weather so I send you greetings

From your Artur. = -
