Großbothen Nov 28th 1939

Dear Vati !

I have read your letter when I came home from school. I was glad to hear from you. How big is your room? I would love to see you again, mothers hand is better again but it still hurts a bit. Did you get the card from Gro8bothen . Either come on Furlough or come home forever, On Sunday night I thought you was home but it was only a dream. Gerhard has covered the clamp a while ago, and today we started pulling turnips. Pusi the cat is catching mice , please come home soon.

Greetings from your Hellmut.
