Eastern Front July 19th 1942

Dear All Beloved !

Today only a short note I am still well, we are now short of Woroshilowgrad and big Mountains and deep Valleys had to be crossed and stong points everywhere even far behind their front. But we are going forward to Victory and to final Victory and God will protect us and march with us.

Heartfelt Greetings Heinrich.

From : Heinrich Bredemeier FP # 10331= Art.Regt 117

To: Family H.Bredemeier , Rehren A.O. 12 nr Rinteln, weser.
