June 5th 1943

Dear Parents.

Greetings from here hope all is well at home, Thank you for the letter, no, we have not heard any enemy Bombers yet, I am on guard today so I write this card at 5 AM. Here all is nice, been eating cherries by the pounds. Any news from home? Let me know, Greetings to you

From Werner.

From: Werner Ketschau, FP # 42691 RAD Labor service in the Warthegau

To: Fam. Erich Ketschau, Klein Olsen , - Erfurt, Thur.
