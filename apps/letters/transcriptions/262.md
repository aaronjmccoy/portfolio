Italy Nov 15th 1944

Dear Comrade Heinz !

I had not heard from you for a long time and as you can see, I am in Italy now near Droino and right now things are quiet here , but it can change from one day to the next. I will not forget the days we had together in Russia . To myself, I was in hospital in Brünn 4 months with a wound on my left arm , a 10 cm gash from a shrapnel but the healing went well and I am back in service again. Let me know where you are I am with a Panzer Abteilung now driving a armored car

Greetings from your old Comrade Kurt.

From: Sgt Kurt Oelmann, FP # 57085 Panzer Regt 130 in Italy

To: Corp. H.Bredemeier, FP # 10331 Art Regt 117

Letter was returned to sender because at the time Heinrich -Bredemeier was serving in Romania under a new FP # so the letter he send then to Bredemeiers home address in Rehren

Sgt Kurt Oelmann b. June 2™ 1912 was killed in Action on April 9th 1945 in northern Italy. Some of his belongings ended up in Bredemeiers posession after the war. Heinrich Bremeier Jr lost a leg in the battle for Upper Silesia and came home in June 1945 .
