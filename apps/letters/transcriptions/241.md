Hungary June 17th 1944

Dear Parents!

Just a few words again writing on the tran in a cattle car, It looks like our train will take us to Romania . I am well, and hope you too. So from far away I have to congratulate you to your Silver Wedding anniversary may God protect you and I hope to see you again. I must now give the letter to the Mil man.

Greetings Heinrich,

From: Corp Heinrich Bredemeier, in Transit.

To: Family Heinrich Bredemeier Rehren A.O. #12 Near Rinteln-Weser.
