July 17th 1941

Dear Artur and All.

Having a few minutes time I will write to you. Please accept my apology for not writing earlier was just impossible. I am well enough and I must not forget, to congratulate you , Dear Artur, on your birthday. My Congratulations come from my heart, I already hat told Lottchen to send you a card. I am very well and I hope all of you are the same. I also the mood at home is full of expectations from Russian news, so I will close now and hope these few words finds you all well. Greetings to Papa, Werner and Mama.

Greetings from Artur.

From: Pvt Artur Dornberger, FP # 19764 = Inf Regt 90

To: Artur Ketschau, Klein Olsen , Erfurt, Thurg.

Artur Dornberger was killed on Oct 3rd 1943 in the Battle for Kiew.
