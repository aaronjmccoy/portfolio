Eastern Front June 21° 1942

Dear Parents and Grand Dad !.

A few days ago I got your letter from June 12 and because it came by Air, it only took 4 days to get here. I am glad about that. I am glad that you are busy now rearing Chickens and doing the Flower Garden I wish I could be with you there and help. A-year it is tomorrow that this war with Russia started and it will not end this year either I am not in the mood to write because there is no change .

Greetings Heinrich.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier FP # 10331 = Art Regt 117

To: Mr Heinrich Bredemeier, Rehren A.O. #12 near Rinteln Weser
