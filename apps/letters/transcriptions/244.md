Romania June 28th 1944

Dear All.

I can not give you an address today our train came to a stop somewhere in the center of Romania and where we end up we don’t know and wait and see. I am well and hope you too what do you think, will the world get fed up with all the war? Greetings sends you

Heinrich.

From: Corp. Heinrich Bredemeier , in transit Romania.

To: Family H.Bredemeier, 20 Rehren A,O. 12 near Rinteln
