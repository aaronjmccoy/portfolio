June 9th 1943

Dear Parents.

Greetings from here and I hope all is well at home. Today only a short note and I can tell you that I got letters # 10 and 11 and parcel # 22 & 23. Even got a few letters from Werner, we have at the moment a fire Pause, and not much to do. I will write always when I have the time to do it, so please do not worry, I will be careful here. Has Werner returned from RAD Labor service? I will write him a letter too later. How is the weeding coming along? Change the oil on the tractor before you start harvesting . Write soon and Greetings to you all,
