June 29th 1943

Dear Parents.

Sending you greetings from here and I hoe all is well . Lately I had a lot of mail from you , parcels # 13-14-15 and letters ad cards from the 18th and 22nd plus letters with Money from June 23™. I also had at last mail from our Artur and he says all is well with him. This week we maybe going to the front but so far we had a nice time here.

Please do not write to this address anymore , I don’t know what else to write so I send you Greetings and lets hope for a reunion.

Your Werner.

From: Werner Ketschau, no FP # supplied

To: Fam.Erich Ketschau,Kleinmdlsen, -Erfurt Thurg.
