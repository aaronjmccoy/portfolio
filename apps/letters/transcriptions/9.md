Großbothen Nov 42nd 1940

Dear Good Vati.

Today we got your card and we thank you. I wished you was at home , I don’t know what you look like , you might be very fat now, and on Monday you was 6 weeks gone. You want to know what I am doing? I run with my skates or use the sledge but on my skates the iron is loose. Yesterday we shoveled snow, because Gerhard had to work. Molly is always happy when we stroke him, today it is snowing again .

Greetings from your Hellmut.
