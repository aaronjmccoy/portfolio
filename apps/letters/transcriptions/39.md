Monday May 26th 1941

Dear beloved All !

This will be my last letter from here that we are permitted to write. Later today we have a weapon inspection but nobody is cleaning, everyone is busy writing we don’t know the reason for all this . Every 15 minutes a fully loaded train passes through here , don’t worry about me, if you don’t hear from me for a while. Just keep on praying that all will go well so I wish you a very happy

Whitsun.

Greetings Heinrich.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier FP # 10331 = Art Regt 117

To: Family H. Bredemeier , Farmer, Rehren A.O. #12 Near

Schaumburg
