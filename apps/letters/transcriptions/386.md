Nov 25th 1944

Dear Parents.

Greetings once again from another area now, We are now near Arys on a Army training ground. He we shall get the final instructions. I hope all is well at home and how far you with work in the fields? Have you harvested the sugar beets?

Lets hope the war will end soon, I don’t think it will last much longer anyway. Greetings to you all and hopefully we see each other soon.

Werner.
