Großbothem Oct 10th 1942

Dear Gerhard.

Thank you for the card and the letter, we thought you would come for a visit because at noon a train came by full of Soldiers and Papa said, someone from the train kept waving. I should have looked too at the train but I didn’t . Today we finish in the Potato field. We can not send you any Cigarettes, sorry, you can put your finger into your mouth and that is as good as a cigarette. They miss you a lot at Wilhelm’s , I send yoy greetings.

Your Hellmut,
