O.U. April 19th 1944

Dear Brother and Sister in Law!

I have to tell you that I had no answer yet to my application, but not to worry, how are you? Are you all well and I good health? We have left Greece now and are in Croatia so that is not far from home really. But I rather would have stayed in Greece , there we could go and buy things and had plenty to eat. But its different here, my salary all I can get here is 2 shots of Schnapps so you know how expensive things are here. Live is no bed on roses either here, to many Partisans around. I enclose you the letter from the council, and it is pointless that I do something about it now. The Circumstances have changed a lot since my application. We both have to provide some extras for mother. So let me know what you think. Wishing you all the best

Hermann.

From; Corp. Hermann Jagel, FP 46631 = Army transport Battalion 993

To: Hugo Jagel, Rastatt/ Baden Hilda Str 21.

Wehrmacht Report from end of April 1944 Croatia and Monte Negro

On account of the good weather , the attacks of Bandits and Partisans increased in Croatia and Monte Negro. The main Rail connection between Agram and Banja Luca was blown in several places, traffic between these two cities will be delayed for 7 hours. Patrols of the 107" Gebirgs Division captured 67 Serbian Saboteurs , and transferred to Belgrade for sentence.
