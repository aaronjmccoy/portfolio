August 31° 1943

Dear parents and Margarete!

Thank you for the letter , here are only a few lines from me. I have mailed you Cigarettes and Tobacco, I hope it all gets there. I shall wait for a while until I send the other things. We were able to buy a lot from the PX Store now and I can send it all at once. Greetings for now.

Erich

From: Corp.Erich Köhler, FP # 08 439 = Grenadier Regt 394

To: Family Richard Kéhler, Chemnitz, Leopoldstr 4
