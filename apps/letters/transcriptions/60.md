Ukraine Sept 24th 1941

Dear Beloved All !

Not much time but I will on this rest day write to you. We are having a rest day because the Horses are to tired You maybe have heard from the Radio, that the Capital City of the Ukraine was taken last week. There were many Prisoners taken and now we are pursuing the rest of the soviet Army. We are now 70 Kilometer east of Kiew. I am well, let me know if you had the money I mailed. I will write more when I have the time, How is the work at home? Is the Tractor going well? How is the Green Fodder from the large meadow? Will there be a good grain harvest? Anything else new? Keep feeding the Horses plenty of Oats I hope to hear from you soon and get some fat again

Your son Heinrich.

Greetings to All Neighbors and tell thenm that I have no time to write.

From: Pvt Heinrich Bredemeier FP # 10331 Art Regt 117

To: Family Heinrich Bredemeier, Rehren A.O. # 12 near Rinteln-Weser.
