Leipzig May 28th 42.

To the Comradeship service organization of the Greater German Radio Transmissions in

Berlin.

Our inquiries to the whereabouts of our son , Heinz Decker, FP # 37757 has brought no response but we have not given up hope that he might be sick in a hospital and we ask you, whether it is possible via the Comradeship Service to search for our son. Maybe there are circumstances that he is unable to give his name , and do you know of any institution that deals with these situations? It could also be possible that a Comrade of our son will hear your search announcement with our address and could contact us whether he was together with our son , or knows him. In any event, we are very grateful if you see to our wishes. We sure hope that we will get a message from our son that way.

Heil Hitler

Hermann Decker, Leipzig W 32

Hermann Meyerstr 41
