Russia, July 7th 1942

Dear Parents

Just a short sign of life I am well and I am top fit too, and hope you too . I have not much time and can not write so often as I want. How are you otherwise and how are the fields? Mail and parcels I had today again and I will answer the letters in a short while. Many thanks to your

congratulations to my Birthday.

Greetings to all. Your Artur.
