April 25th 1943

Dear Parents!

Greetings once again from me, I hope you are well and in good health so I am here. I just had mail from you today , letter # 25 and Parcels # 24 & 25. Thank you very much.

Today is Easter and a beautiful day it is. This morning we had a Church Field Service and last night we were shown a movie, naturally they showed the film, “ The Delousing”, this afternoon we got Schnapps and we had a good drinking party. I suppose at home the weather is good too, and you busy with potato planting and turnip sowing. How is Werner, I very seldom get mail from him. I will close now, the Schnapps is affecting my writing and I am sending Greetings and lets hope for a reunion soon

Artur.

From: Pvt 1st Class Artur Ketschay, FP # 21764F=Pz Grenadier Regt 112

To: Family Erich Ketschau,KleinmGlsen, Erfurt-Thurg.
