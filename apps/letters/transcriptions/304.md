Feb 22™ 4) Z

Dear Parents,

We are still in the Station at Orscha , last night we had a air raid with bombing , the track is blown again, when the bombs came down, we jumped into a trench, thesere are many along the tracks for us to use.it is cold again . Greetings Artur,
