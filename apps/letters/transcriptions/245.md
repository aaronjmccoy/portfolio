Romania June 29th 1944

Dear Parents !

I am still not wiser than when I wrote last, and sending greetings from far away. We still waiting in the rail Road station for something?, and wonder were we will be going. I am well, Greetings

Heinrich

From: pvt H. Bredemeier FP # 10331 Art Regt 117 in transit

To: Family Bredemeier, Rehren, A.O. #12 near Rinteln Weser
