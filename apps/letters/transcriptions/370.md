In the East Sept 7th 1944 Dear Parents!

Thank you for the letter # 17 and I sand you greetings from here. Lets hope all is well with you like it is with me. Who has helped in turnip harvest? Have the Ketschau’s from Udestedt come to help or don’t they come anymore? I suppose that lazy lot will not turn up. How is the mood at home and do people think about the bad situations at the fronts? We all hope and wait for the end to come. Hope to see you soon at home.

Your Werner
