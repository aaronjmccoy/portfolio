March 20th 1943

Dear Parents

Today is time to write again, and send you greetings from here. I am well as well can be said and hope at home too. So far I have had no mail from you but there is mail for us at the Company office. Some of it was send back to you, the earlier letters because at the time I was unknown here so please write the FP # clearly, 21 764 F you can ask at your P.O. why that happened too. Any other news from home yet? How is Werner, started on the fields yet? Aare any on furlough, the restrictions have lifted here now. If you can, send mea few cigarettes, I have nothing now.

For today I will close now and please write soon , so I send Greetings to all of you and lets hope we see each other again.

Artur,

From: Pvt 1‘ Class Artur Ketschay, FP # 21764F=Pz Grenadier Regt 112

To: Family Erich Ketschau,KleinmGlsen, Erfurt-Thurg.
