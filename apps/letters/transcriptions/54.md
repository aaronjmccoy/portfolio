July 31 1941

Dear All.

I just got 3 nice long letters from you and the contend was very interesting . Here all is well , please do not worry so much about me , I am ok, it will look funny to other people.

Greetings Heinrich

From: Pvt H. Bredemeier FP # 10331 = Artillery Regt 117

To: Fam. Heinrich Bredemeier , Rehren, A.O. # 12, Near Rinteln.
