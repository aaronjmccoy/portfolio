Mech Aleksander

B: Feb 23™ 1918

Prisoner # 5181 Block 16 ST 4 Dachau K3

Dachau Nov 24th 1940

Dear Mutti.

Last letter never came, Dear Sisters, if you make loads of money, send a parcel, what is it that makes Mutti sick? Same as last year or is it better? You must see a Doctor, maybe the heath care pays for it. I am well and I pray each day to God for your health, dear Mutti, write to me what happens in our village and with all our friends. Are our Cousins Stefan and

Constantine back from Kärnten? How is the Family? Please write soon, Greetings to you all and the Kascynski and Ruriseki Family and to you I send Greetings and Kisses to our Sisters , to Mutti and Comrades.

Your beloved Aleksander
