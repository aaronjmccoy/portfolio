Wednesday Oct 8th 1941

Dear All.

Writing a short note and hope that all is well at home and the same goes forme. As Lotte has told me, Artur was drafted too on Oct 34 and is in Jena. That means that you will have one worker less on the farm to lift potatoes and beets, and who knows who will help in Udestedt now. Yes I can see what will happen to all of us, maybe you can send me Arturs address in Jena . For now I will close and hope to see you soon again.

Many Greetings to Artur

Your Artur

From: Pvt 1st Class Artur Dornberger FP # 19 764 = Inf Regt 90

To: Mr Erich Ketschau, Klein Olsen , - Erfurt, Thur.
