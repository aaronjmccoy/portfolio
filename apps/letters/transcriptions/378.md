In the East Sept 27th 1944

My Dear Parents.

Greetings from here, hope all is well at home, I did not get mail yesterday but I send you a parcel with Cigarettes and Tobacco. I don’t smoke often, Cigars and tobacco is for Papa, the Cigarettes you go with as you like. Yesterday we had a film in a barn about 6 miles behind the front line, it was the movie , Antony the Last”.

Greetings to you from Werner

From: Pvt 1st Class Werner Ketschau, FP # 27658= Panzer Grenadier Rgt 6

To:Family Erich Ketschau, Kleinmmmmm148lsen, Erfurt -Thurg.

Panzer Grenadier Regt 6 was a Regt of the 7th Panzer Division , the original 7th Panzer Div. was Commanded by General Erwin Rommel .

By Sept 1944, at the time Werner Ketschau wrote this letter, the Division was Commanded be General D. Mauss and was fighting in Lithuania around the town of Wirballen.
