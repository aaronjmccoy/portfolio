June 14th 1943

Dear Brother in law.

Time to write to you once again, and thank you for the 3 parcels, but please send the Cigarettes to Artur, I don’t smoke much now. Artur had written to me on May 31* and according to his letter, he is in a quiet area and he said he is very well, I myself are with the supply battalion at the moment and far from the front. I am sitting here doing nothing and do not know , how to pass the time . I do not want to think of home and all the work that has to be done there. I can imagine what a job it is to get on with these Prisoners of war working at the farm. Let us hope that idiotic war soon will come to an end. I suppose you at home are like us here, we all had enough of it and want to go home. That is all the news from here, let us hope for a speedy end and reunion.

Artur.

From: Corp. Artur Dornberger, FP # 19 764 = Inf . Regt 90

To: Family Erich Ketschau, Kleinmélsen Erfurt Thurg

Artur Dornberger Born: July 10th 1910 , Died on October 3 1943 nea Kiew -Ukraine.
