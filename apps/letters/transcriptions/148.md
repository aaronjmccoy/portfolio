R October 31* 1942

Dear All ! I just received the letter fro Oct 29th I see you are well so am I. Today I wrote a long letter to Major Lange , he wanted some questions answers but with a transfer to him it is impossible as I have to stay here at least 4 weeks and then I can think about it. My only wish will be, not to send there where I came from, once I am back in the Braunschweig camp, you have to do something to get me home. Rather I go without all the things from home and maybe Major Lange can do something for me. I am well looked after her and have no urge to get into a hospital in Hamlin. About the watch: there are some to buy or in a exchange for Gold or Silver maybe in exchange for a silver Coffee pot. I don’t think Major Lange can hel me there. Well, you have to try.

From: H. Bredemeier Rothenburg o.d.T. Hospital Bären

To: Fam. Heinrich Bredemeier , Rehren, A.O. # 12, Near Rinteln.
