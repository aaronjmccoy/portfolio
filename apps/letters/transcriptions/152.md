Sunday Nov 29th 1942 Dear All!

I am writing laying in bed I shall not get out that early from Hospital, I got Jaundice and I will be here in bed over Christmas too. It is even a hard job to write. I like the letter from Gerd Becker, I can understand now that they miss their old point rider on the gun. The Horses will miss me too. All 4 of us were good friends, Gelmann is home on furlough, say hello to him from me. May be I have to stay in Hospital as long as he did. Send me a few photos from home too, tomorrow I will mail a pipe and tobacco for Grand dad. The parcel got here yesterday but I am not allowed to eat sausage and I can not eat much anyway. The photo with the horse , that is me.

From: Pvt 1st Class H,Bredemeier Rothenburg o.d.t. Hospital Bären

To: Fam. Heinrich Bredemeier , Rehren, A.O. # 12, Near Rinteln.
