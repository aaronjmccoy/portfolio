Großbothen June 9th 1940

Dear Vati.

Just a quick few words , today we are in the common meadow cutting hay, Mr Guntschka and Mr Greig are helping too, our own meadow will be next. The Wettzelts have their hay already in . The little cats are nice, they fighting with each other , Dear Vati, I am sending you a photo that was taken last Sunday . Greetings from Grand ma and Grand Dad too.

Greetings Your Hellmut.
