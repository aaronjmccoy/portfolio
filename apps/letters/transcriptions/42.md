O.U. June 13th 1941

Dear All at home !

We getting nearer and nearer to our destination and today is aday of rest but only by name. It is 10 PM as I write , I enclose some money and please bank it for me . Be patient, soon you will be hearing the latest news and happenings , so lets all wait and see. Stay well, that is all for now,

Your Heinrich

From. Pvt H.Bredemeier Fp # 10331 Art Regt 117

To: Family H. Bredemeier , Rehren A.O. #12 Near Rinteln -Weser

On June 22™ , The German Army attacked Russia.
