March 4th 1943

Dear Parents .

Please send me pencil when you can get one, I have lost mine. It is midnight here when I sit down and write this letter, and sending greetings to you and hope all is well. The weather is the same every day here, looks like it will be like last spring. Have you heard from Artur Dornberger? Please send me his address again.have you got the photos now? If so, send me one please. How is Werner? Have you taken the filly out to play lately? Write soon please Greetings from

Artur.

From: Pvt Artur Ketschau, FP # 21764 = Panzer Grenadier Rgt 112

To: Family Erich Ketschau, Kleeinmélsen-Erfurt Thurg.
