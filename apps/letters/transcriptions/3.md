Großbothen Dec 12th 1939

Dear Papa.

Thank you for the Chocolate and the Pfeffer Cake. How is things with the Soldier men then? Have you got a room for you alone? Have you opend the parcel yet or will you wait until Christmas Eve. That is not far off now. Greetings to you

Hellmut
