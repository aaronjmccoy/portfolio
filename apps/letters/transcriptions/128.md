Eastern Front July 27th 1942

Dear Parents and All !

Today I got more of the small parcels, and also letter and thank you very much. We are all well, Rostov was taken by our troops and now we have to wait to see where we goint to next. I enclose a 1 Kilo Parcel stamp and send me a can of Sugar beet Syrup. Greetings Heinrich.

From: Pvt 1st Class Heinrich Bredenmeier, FP # 10331 = Artillery Regt 117

To: Family Bredenmeier Rehren AV 12 near Rinteln /Weser.
