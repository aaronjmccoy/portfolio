Russia Dec 12th 1941

Dear All at home !

Today I got mail again and everything I wanted arrived. I got enough now to last for a while , I am well and hope the same from you . Today we occupy a school house. The declaration of war to America came as a surprise , don’t you think so? It means a few more years of war now. Just keep on writing, Once again thank you for the good things. , I will close now your Heinrich.
