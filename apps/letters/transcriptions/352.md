Dec 13th 1943

Dear Parents.

Have to give you the bad news ,that I am on the list of those that will be send to the front. Let us hope and pray for the best. Tomorrow morning we shall be examined by the Doctor . I try my best to see if I can get a weekend furlough before I go.

Many Greetings from your

Werner

75 of us are being transferred.

From: Pvt Werner Ketschau, FP # 56 999 = Reserve Grenadier Battalion 71

To: Fam. Erich Ketschau, Kleinmdlsen, -Erfurt/ Thurg
