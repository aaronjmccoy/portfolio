In the East Aug 18th 1944

Dear Parents.

Greetings from far away, hope all is well at home, today we will be taken out of the front line but we do not know where we going from here. Do not worry about me, even if you don’t get mail for a time, so for now, I send you my Greetings and see you at home one day.

Your Werner
