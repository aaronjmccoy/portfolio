Russia March 8th 1943

Dear Parents.

Once again 2 AM here and I just returned from guard duty. Now I have the time to write a short letter, with Greetings from here and I am well. The weather is getting better, it had started thawing and it is not cold anymore. What is new at home? How are they in Udestedt? Did you had mail from Artur? Let me know his address please. I will close now, lets hear from you.

Greetings Artur.

From:Pvt 1st Class Artur Ketschau, FP # 21764 = Grenadier Regt 112

To: fam Erich Ketschau, Klein Olsen - Erfurt Thur.
