France. Sept 26° 1943

Dear Parents,

Greetings from here , hope all is well at home, as I am too.

We have rain every day here what is the weather doing at home and how far you with work? Have you started on the potatoes? Everything well at home? Greetings until next time and a reunion from your

Werner.

From: Pvt Werner Ketschau, FP # 56999 = Reserve Grenadier Battalion 71

To: Family Erich Ketschau, Kleinmélsen- Erfurt, Thurg.
