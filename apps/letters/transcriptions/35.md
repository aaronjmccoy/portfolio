Berlin May 16th 1941

Dear beloved All !

I have a stopover of 3 hrs in Berlin and me and a Comrade we are here in Charlottenburg Station with Coffee , There was an alert on but it only lasted a short while. We are talking about our furlough, it is a long night for us. We might arrive tonight there so I send you greetings.

Heinrich.
