Mauthausen October 25th 1943

Dear Wife!

I can tell you that I am in good health and feel very well. I thank you for the parcel , the letter and the money. Please send me money and parcels often, why do not write more about our child, and what is going on? Sooner or later I shall get to know what is going on at home. I send Greetings to the Owsick Family, to Master Znyk , greetings and Kisses to my Family and Relatives and Hela, and Cesiek are they still at the old place?

Your Andrzy

From: Borowy Andrzy, B: Nov 9th 1913, Prisoner # 5031 Block 18, Room 17 \ 2 —~

To: Mrs Bora Marta, Kutno, Alter Markt 19/3 Wartheland,

AN —
