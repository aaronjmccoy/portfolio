Eastern Front Sept 1° 1942.

Dear Beloved All !

You will be waiting to hear from me yes, since July 1* we are on the advance again and we seldom have a quiet rest somewhere. Because we travel at night , we rather sleep in the few daylight hours than writing but please do not worry if you don’t hear from me for a while, we can do nothing about it. It looks like our home thid winter will be the Caucasus Mountains, but our hopes is furlough too. How far are you with the Harvest? Anyone from us here coming back home can tell you what it means to be home again. These last few weeks on the advance we did 1128

Kilometers I don’t look at the map anymore, because I don’t know where we going anyway.

Greetings Heinrich

From: Pvt 1st Class Heinrich Bredemeier , FP # 10331 = Art Regt 117

To: Family Heinrich Bredemeier, Rehren A.O. # 12 near Rinteln Weser.
