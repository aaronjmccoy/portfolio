Ukraine Aug 1° 1941

Dear All at Home !

These last few days I got many letters from you and I send letters or cards home almost daily. But don’t think I will keep this up, I am not having the time to write so often , right now we have a few days rest . Normally we are on the road most of the time. I suppose the harvest is in full swing at home now . We just crossing parts of the fields that will not be harvested , I am well, keep on writing so we have a connection with home. With my best wishes for Victory in August

Heinrich.
