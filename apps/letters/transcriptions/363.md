In the East June 27th 1944

Dear Parents.

Greetings from here and hope all is well, I had no mail today , we have very nice weather and I hope it is like that at home. I made potato soup today, was really good . In the Army one learns a lot it seems. I even build myself a stove to cook on. Well, that is all for now. Hope to see you one day.

Greetings Werner

Werner Ketschau was in several units, but all were Grenadier Sharpshooters Companies, with different FP #.
