June 6th 1944

Dear Parents and Grand dad.

Today you will get my last letter from here , I was ordered back to Braunschweig and I have to be ready for the next transport back to the Eastern Front. I am on it, in an hour the train will leave for Braunschweig, and I will write more later.

Heinrich.
