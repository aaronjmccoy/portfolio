Romania Nov 20th 1944

Dear Ketschau Family.

Firstly, belated I send you my condolences to the death of your Brother in Law Artur in Oct 43. At the time of the incidend, I was in Company II under 1* Lt Peters . It is now over a year ago and memories have faded a bit since then but it happened late evening on Oct 3rd about 11 PM during an Artillery shelling by the enemy when his Bunker sustained a direct hit. &’of our Comrades were killed , amongst them my Comrade Artur ,

We lost a very good Comrade . The Staff Sgt of the 3rd Platoon at the time must have send Arturs Private belongings to the

Dornberger Family.

A picture I enclose, Artur is 2nd from the left.

With my best Greetings

Karl Klüters

From: Karl Klüters FP # 19964 = Artillery Regt77

To; Family E. Ketschau, Kleinmölsen, Erfurt, Thurg.
