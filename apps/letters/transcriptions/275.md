Feb 28th 1942

Dear Werner.

Will write a few short words to you, I am well and hope you too. The winter is less colder now, but the nights are cold. There will be Spring at home by now I suppose and work will start in the fields. Artur will miss it too and I am not there either. Dear Werner, I was promoted to Corp on Feb 1* but that is all the news I have from here. So I close in the hope to see you again one day , greetings to you from

Artur. D

Greetings to your Mama and Papa.
