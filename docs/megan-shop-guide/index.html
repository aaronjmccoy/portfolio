Finishing a listing

For each painting:

  1. In Shopify, go to Products and click the painting. Shopify help
  2. Title: the painting's name as you want it shown.
  3. Description: delete both placeholder lines and write the real one. Worth including:
    • size in inches, width × height, and the framed size if framed
    • medium and surface
    • year
    • framed, ready to hang, or unframed
    • signed, and where
    • a sentence or two about the piece
  4. Media, the photos. Shopify help
    • The first photo is the one in the grid and the big one on the page. Make it the whole painting, straight on, cropped to the edge of the canvas, in even light with no glare.
    • After it: close-ups of texture and brushwork, the edges, the signature, the back, the painting on a wall for scale.
    • Drag photos to reorder them. Whatever is first becomes the main photo.
    • At least 2048 pixels on the long side. Shopify's limit is 25 megapixels and 20 MB per photo: 4000 × 5000 is 20 megapixels and fine, 6000 × 6000 is 36 and will be refused. JPEG, PNG, WEBP and HEIC all work.
  5. Alt text on every photo: click the photo, click Add alt text, type a short description, then Save alt text. Describe what's in the picture in under 125 characters, for example "Black cockatoo in oils, head turned over its shoulder, gold flecks across dark blue feathers" or "Detail of the eye and pale beak". Screen readers read it aloud to people who can't see the image. Shopify help
  6. Price: the real price. Leave the compare-at price empty.
  7. Inventory: turn on Track quantity and set Available to 1. Leave the out-of-stock setting on Stop selling when out of stock. Each painting then sells once and shows as Sold. Shopify help
  8. Shipping: the packed weight, painting plus packaging. Weigh it boxed if you can.
  9. Product organization: already filled in on these nine. It should read Type Original, Vendor Maller Art, Collections Paintings, Tags original. Leave it.
  10. Search engine listing: click the pencil and check the URL handle reads well, like three-brothers. Settle it once. After prints of a painting exist, don't change its handle; the prints are tied to it. Shopify help
  11. Publishing: make sure Headless is in the list. Headless is the new site; a painting not published there won't appear.
  12. Status: Active once everything is real. Draft keeps it off the site.
  13. Click Save.
If a painting is too large or fragile for a normal courier, tell Aaron before you set it to Active so its shipping can be arranged.

Adding a new painting

The quickest way is to copy a finished painting, so the organisation fields come along:

  1. In Products, open any finished painting.
  2. Click More actions, then Duplicate product. Shopify help
  3. Type the new painting's title, choose Draft as the status, and click Duplicate product.
  4. On the copy, delete the old photos, then work through steps 3 to 13 above.
  5. Check Product organization still reads Original / Maller Art / Paintings / original.

You can also start from Products → Add product. Then fill in Product organization yourself: Type Original, Vendor Maller Art, Collections Paintings, Tags original.

If the new painting should have prints, add a row for it to your print sizes table (2b).

2. Prints with Prodigi

How it works. You choose the paper and, for each painting, the print sizes and prices. Aaron then creates all the print listings in Shopify at once, one per painting, called "Painting name Print", with a size option for each size you chose. Once they exist, there's a second part to this guide for setting them up with Prodigi and making them live. Until then, the three steps below are all there is to do for prints.

2a. Choose the paper (once)

  1. Order Prodigi's fine art sample pack from your Prodigi account: £10 plus tax, free shipping, sent from their US facility. It has their bestselling papers printed with stock images. Prodigi samples
  2. Compare the papers in daylight next to the originals. The ones worth comparing for paintings: Prodigi paper guide
    • Hahnemühle German Etching, 310 gsm: velvety matte, textured, warm white
    • Hahnemühle Photo Rag, 308 gsm: smooth matte cotton, light felt texture
    • Cold press watercolour, 315 gsm: matte cotton, strong texture
    • Enhanced Matte Art, 200 gsm: smooth matte, natural white, no optical brighteners; the budget option
  3. Order one real print of one painting on your top two papers. Prodigi refunds half of one sample per product type if you email the order number to support@prodigi.com.

2b. Choose print sizes and prices for each painting

Print sizes have to match the painting's shape or the print gets cropped. Measure the painting, divide its width by its height, and pick print sizes that give the same number:

  • a 16 × 20 in painting is 0.8, so 8 × 10 and 16 × 20 keep the whole image
  • a 12 × 18 in painting is 0.67, so 8 × 12, 12 × 18 and 20 × 30 keep it
  • a square painting needs square sizes

Check the sizes exist on your chosen paper by searching Prodigi's catalogue in the dashboard; it also shows Prodigi's price for each size. Your price has to cover that plus shipping.

Fill in each painting below. Type sizes in inches with the width first, like 12 × 18 or 12x18, and prices in US dollars. A print size that doesn't match the painting's shape is flagged so you can fix it before anything is printed.

Leave a painting with no print sizes if it shouldn't have prints. To remove a print size, clear it.

2c. Prepare the print files

Get these ready now; you'll need them when the listings are set up with Prodigi. The print file is not the listing photo. It needs a high-resolution scan or photograph of the painting. Prodigi file guide

  • Resolution: 300 DPI at the largest size you sell. A 16 × 20 in print needs 4800 × 6000 pixels. A photo 4000 pixels wide only covers about 13 inches.
  • Colour: RGB.
  • Format: JPG is safest. Prodigi takes JPG, PNG or PDF, but some papers only JPG or PDF.
  • One file usually covers every size of the same shape. Prodigi's uploader shows the pixel size each product wants and warns if a file is too small.
  • Prodigi's product pages offer ICC profiles. They're for checking a proof against a real print, not for on-screen soft proofing.

3. Trinkets with Printful

Trinkets is everything that isn't a painting or a print: stickers, shirts, hoodies, hats, totes and the rest.

3a. Prepare the artwork

  • PNG with a transparent background. A JPG prints as a white box.
  • sRGB colour, the profile called sRGB IEC61966-2.1.
  • Resolution: at least 150 DPI at the printed size; 300 DPI for stickers, phone cases and DTFlex products. No gain above 300.
  • No soft edges. Semi-transparent edges, fades and drop shadows print unevenly on fabric.
  • Each product in Printful's catalogue has a File guidelines tab with its print area and rules. Check it before designing.
  • On blends and heathers the fabric shows through the ink and mutes colour. Plain 100% cotton gives the truest colour with DTG.

3b. DTG or DTFlex?

The two ways Printful prints a full-colour image on clothing: Printful comparison

DTG (direct to garment)DTFlex (Printful's direct-to-film)
HowInk printed straight into the fabricPrinted on film, then heat-pressed on top
FeelSoft, almost no feel, breathesSlightly thicker and raised, less breathable on big prints
Fabrics100% cottonCotton, polyester, fleece, nylon, blends, hats
ColourGood, with fine detail and gradientsThe most vivid
WashingFades graduallyHolds colour longest; if it fails, edges crack or lift
Watch forDuller colour on blendsBlack on black fabric comes out dark grey

When bright, lasting colour matters most, choose DTFlex. When the shirt is cotton and softness matters most, choose DTG. DTFlex is only on some products; Printful's catalogue has a DTFlex products collection, including the Unisex Staple T-Shirt, Unisex Premium Hoodie and several caps. If unsure, sample the same design both ways.

3c. Make the product in Printful

Printful help

  1. In the Printful Dashboard, go to Stores and click Add product. Some accounts show My products → Create product instead. Choose the Maller Art store if asked.
  2. Pick the product from the catalogue.
  3. Click Start designing to open the Design Maker. Upload your file and position it.
  4. Choose the colours and sizes to offer, then Proceed to mockups.
  5. Pick the mockups customers will see: basic plain backgrounds or styled scenes.
  6. Product details: Printful fills in a description. Add the painting's name and a line about it. A tidy title pattern is the painting plus the item: "Flaunt Tee", "Starstuff Sticker".
  7. Pricing: Printful shows its cost and a suggested price; set your retail price. Printful's cost excludes shipping and tax. Leave Free shipping on my storefront off unless you and Aaron agree otherwise, because shipping is charged in Shopify.
  8. Click Submit to store.

3d. Finish it in Shopify

  1. In Shopify, go to Products and open the new item.
  2. Set Status to Draft while you order a sample from Printful.
  3. In Product organization, click the + next to Collections and choose Trinkets. Shopify help
  4. In Publishing, make sure Headless is in the list.
  5. Click Save.
  6. When the sample is right, set Status to Active and Save.

To add or remove colours or sizes later, do it in Printful, not Shopify, so the two stay in step.
